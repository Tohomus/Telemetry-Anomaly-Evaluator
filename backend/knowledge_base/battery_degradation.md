Battery Degradation

SEVERITY:

Medium

DESCRIPTION:

Gradual deterioration of battery performance resulting in lower voltage output, reduced efficiency, and increasing operating temperature over an extended period.

POSSIBLE CAUSES:

1- Battery aging
2- Repeated charge-discharge cycles
3- Internal chemical degradation
4- Cell imbalance

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:

1- Perform battery health diagnostics
2- Monitor long-term voltage trends
3- Check battery capacity
4- Schedule replacement if necessary
