Current Surge

SEVERITY:

High

DESCRIPTION:

An unexpected increase in current draw. The event may also produce a slight voltage drop and moderate temperature increase due to increased electrical load.

POSSIBLE CAUSES:

1- Payload overload
2- Power subsystem malfunction
3- Short circuit
4- Sudden subsystem activation

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:

1- Inspect power subsystem
2- Verify battery condition
3- Monitor temperature response
4- Isolate high-current components if required
