Cooling Failure

SEVERITY:

High

DESCRIPTION:

The thermal management system is unable to dissipate heat effectively, causing continuous temperature increase and possible voltage degradation.

POSSIBLE CAUSES:

1- Cooling subsystem malfunction
2- Blocked heat dissipation
3- Fan or coolant failure
4- Environmental overheating

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS:

1- Inspect cooling subsystem
2- Reduce system workload
3- Monitor thermal behavior continuously
4- Verify thermal sensors
